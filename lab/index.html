Recover the three golden signals by jumping close to them. Restore Interfaces, Systems and AI in any order, then return to the central Nexus and press E. Systems includes two barriers, three relays and a patrol drone. Fire at the relays to open the route, then connect the final terminal. Interfaces carries an observation through its review route; Applied AI calibrates, tracks moving samples and synchronizes its analysis. Jump over the pulse crossings or wait for their safe phase. The map can guide you to every destination. On touch screens use the arrows, jump and impulse buttons. Magnetic deck edges keep you safe.

02 / SYSTEMS · FIELD MISSION

Relay Breach.

An isolated service deck has lost its connection. Release the barriers, get past its patrol drone, and bring the terminal back online.

  1. Activate three relaysLook for the amber rings. Fire with F or click directly on a target. Blaster range: 5.5 m.
  2. Find your routeMove around the cargo crates. Each relay opens a barrier and saves a checkpoint.
  3. Handle the patrolUse cover, jump or Q to dodge its warning shot. Three hits disable the drone; defeating it is optional.
  4. Reconnect the systemReach the final terminal and connect Browser → API → Database.
